Did she return to her own birthplace?
After decades at sea, this green turtle finds her natal beach. Under cover of darkness, she crawls ashore, guided by ancient instincts.
Away from the waves, she digs a body pit, then carefully carves a deeper egg chamber with her rear flippers.
Around one hundred soft eggs fall into the chamber. Hidden beneath the sand, their future holds a remarkable secret: temperature determines whether they become male or female.
She buries the eggs, disguises the nest, and returns to the sea at dawn, leaving them to develop alone.
Weeks later, the hatchlings emerge together, instinctively climbing through the sand toward the surface and their first dangerous journey.
Following the brightest natural horizon, they rush toward the ocean. Only an estimated one in a thousand survives.
One tiny female swims into the unknown. If she survives, she may someday return to this very beach.
