Under moonlight, a sea turtle returns.
She crawls slowly up the beach, dragging her heavy body across the sand. Tonight, she must find a safe place to nest.
Using her powerful flippers, she clears a body pit, then carefully digs a deep chamber for her eggs.
One by one, soft white eggs drop into the sand. A green sea turtle can lay around one hundred eggs in a single nest.
She covers the nest, hiding it beneath the sand, then returns to the ocean as dawn breaks.
Weeks later, the sand begins to move. Tiny hatchlings push upward, breaking free from their hidden nest.
They race toward the brighter ocean horizon, guided by natural light. Danger surrounds every tiny step.
One reaches the waves and swims into the open sea. A new journey begins.
